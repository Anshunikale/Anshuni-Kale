The idea

Problem
100% of material testing was physical: build, break, repeat. Every round cost labor and material.
What I did
Put simulation in the loop so physical tests only confirm what the model predicts, and test data refines the model.
Simulation replaces build-and-break rounds

How I ran it

STEP 1

Calculate & CAD

Modeled a DRS wing on an inverted NACA 2408 airfoil and computed the load it sees.

21.14 N · 3,675 Pa
STEP 2

Formulate resin

Back-calculated resin targets and fixed a thermal defect by reformulating.

+2 PHR envirospheres
STEP 3

Test properties

Measured stiffness, shear, and thermal expansion so the model is realistic.

α = 0.335 μm/m·°C
STEP 4

Simulate

Ran coupled structural and thermal ANSYS models; found where to reinforce.

converged on first run
